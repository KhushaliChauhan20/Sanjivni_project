Quality Assurance at Sanjivani Industries

At Sanjivani Industries, quality is at the core of everything we do. We manufacture precision casting parts, forging parts, and investment casting components that meet global industry standards and customer expectations.

Our quality control team ensures every part goes through strict inspections — from raw material testing to final dimensional and surface checks. Using advanced CMM, NDT, and metallurgical testing, we guarantee strength, accuracy, and reliability in every product.

We follow ISO-based quality systems and maintain full traceability for all production batches. Continuous improvement, process optimization, and customer satisfaction define our work philosophy.

Trust Sanjivani Industries for consistent quality, precision engineering, and dependable performance in every casting and forging solution.
